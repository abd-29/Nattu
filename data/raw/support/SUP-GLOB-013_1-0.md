# Global support handover template

**Référence :** SUP-GLOB-013
**Version :** 1.0
**Date d'application :** 2026-06-13
**Département :** Support
**Classification :** Internal
**Statut :** En vigueur
**Langue :** en

## 1. Purpose

This document supports cross-region NovaTech service operations.

## 2. Key instruction

The handover must include customer impact, current status, actions performed and next owner.

## 3. Evidence collection

Collect logs, firmware revision, timestamps and serial number before destructive actions.

## 4. Escalation

Escalate unresolved critical cases according to the current support matrix.
