## 1. Purpose

This document supports NovaTech engineering and service teams for international deployments.

## 2. Key requirement

The diagnostic endpoint is read-only and exposed under /v1/diagnostics.

## 3. Operational guidance

Follow the approved configuration baseline and record any field deviation in the service ticket.

## 4. Traceability

Keep serial number, firmware revision and intervention date with the service record.
